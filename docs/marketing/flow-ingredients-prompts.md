# Flow Ingredients — still prompts (lock look)

Generate once (Imagen / Gemini image / Flow still), download PNG, re-upload to every Google Flow account.

Use **plain / soft studio background** for character. Square or 3:4 OK; crop face clear.

Photorealistic portrait of a Malay Malaysian man age 34, short neat black hair,
light stubble, warm brown skin, wearing a dark charcoal polo shirt, standing
chest-up against a plain warm beige background, soft restaurant-tungsten key light,
neutral worried expression looking slightly off camera, sharp eyes, natural skin texture,
no logo, no text, no watermark, high detail, 85mm lens look.
```

Close-up photorealistic of a customer's hand holding a modern smartphone over a
wooden cafe table, screen showing a simple QR code and menu UI in orange accents,
shallow depth of field, warm night cafe lighting, no brand watermark, no extra logos.
```
